🔧 Troubleshooting Tips
If Sync is Failing:
  • Check tablet internet connectivity (WiFi/mobile data)
  • Verify Survey Solutions server is accessible
  • Ensure tablets have sufficient storage space
  • Check for pending supervisor rejections
  • Restart Survey Solutions Interviewer app
If Validation Errors are High:
  • Review questionnaire design and validation rules
  • Provide additional enumerator training
  • Check for systematic data entry issues
  • Review field notes for context
